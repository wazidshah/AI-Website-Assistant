How it works

Up and running in three steps

No API servers to manage, no monthly fees. Everything runs through your WordPress install.

1

Connect your API key

Get a free Google Gemini API key from Google AI Studio (no credit card needed) and paste it into the plugin settings.

2

Train on your content

Click "Start Training". The plugin crawls your pages, posts, and products, chunks them intelligently, and stores semantic embeddings in your database.

3

Go live instantly

A floating chat bubble appears on every page. Visitors can ask questions and get accurate, sourced answers — 24/7, automatically.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built specifically for WordPress — integrates with your existing content out of the box.

🧠

Semantic Search (RAG)

Uses Gemini Embeddings + cosine similarity to find the most relevant chunks of your content before generating an answer.

💬

Conversation Memory

Remembers the thread of conversation within each session for natural, context-aware back-and-forth.

🎨

Fully Customisable

Change the bot name, greeting, primary colour, font, and profile picture directly from the WordPress dashboard.

⚡

Fast & Lightweight

Response caching, rate limiting, and a minimal JS bundle keep your site fast and your API quota protected.

🔒

Privacy Friendly

Visitor questions are never stored in your database. Only your website content chunks live locally.

Admin-Only Mode

💡

Suggested Questions

Add clickable quick-reply buttons to the chat widget to guide visitors and surface your most important content.

Installation guide

Step-by-step setup

From zero to live chatbot in under 5 minutes.

1

Install the plugin

Install the plugin by downloading the latest zip from GitHub Releases and uploading it via Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin in your WordPress dashboard.

💡 You can also clone the repository directly into your wp-content/plugins/ folder and run composer install.
2

Get a free Gemini API key

Visit aistudio.google.com, sign in with your Google account, and create a new API key. Copy it to your clipboard.

✅ The free tier of Gemini 2.0 Flash includes generous rate limits — more than enough for most websites.
3

Configure the plugin

In your WordPress admin, go to Settings → AI Assistant. Paste your API key, choose a model, set your bot name and greeting, then click Save Settings.

# Settings → AI Assistant
API Key: AIza••••••••••••••••••••
Model: Gemini 2.0 Flash (Recommended)
Bot Name: Your Site Assistant
Greeting: Hi! How can I help you today?
4

Train on your content

Go to the Training tab. Click Start Training. The plugin will discover all your pages, posts, and products via your sitemap, then crawl and embed them one by one. Watch the progress bar fill up.

⏱ Training time depends on your content size. A typical 50-page site takes about 2–3 minutes. Retrain whenever you update your content.
5

Customise & go live

Optional: add your custom system prompt, set a brand colour, upload a bot avatar, and add suggested questions. When ready, uncheck Admin-Only Mode to make the chat widget visible to all visitors.

# Example custom system prompt
You are a helpful assistant for {site_name}.
Answer questions based only on the content provided.
Always mention our contact page if you're unsure.
FAQ

Common questions

Everything you might wonder before installing.

Is this plugin completely free?
Yes. The plugin is free and open source (GPL-2.0). The only cost is your Google Gemini API usage, which has a generous free tier — Gemini 2.0 Flash is free up to 1,500 requests per day. Most small to medium websites will never need to pay.
Will the AI make things up or "hallucinate"?
The plugin uses RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) — it only passes your actual website content to Gemini as context. The system prompt instructs the AI to only answer from that context and to say "I'm not sure" if information isn't available, significantly reducing hallucination risk.
Do visitor conversations get stored anywhere?
No. Visitor questions and AI responses are never stored in your database. Only your website content chunks (what the chatbot learns from) are stored locally. Conversation history is held in the browser session only.
How do I keep the chatbot up to date?
Just go back to the Training tab and click "Start Training" again whenever you update your content. This clears the old index and rebuilds it fresh from your current pages and posts.
Can I control what the chatbot does and doesn't answer?
Yes. Use the custom System Prompt field in Settings to give the AI specific instructions — define its tone, restrict topics, require it to recommend your contact page for certain queries, and more. The {site_name} token is automatically replaced at runtime.
Does it work with WooCommerce product pages?
Yes. Enable WooCommerce Products under Settings → Crawler & Indexing, then retrain. Product descriptions, prices, and attributes will be indexed and the chatbot can answer product-related questions.
Can I use a different AI model?
Yes. The model dropdown in Settings fetches a real-time list of available Gemini models from the API. You can switch between Gemini 2.0 Flash (fast, free) and more powerful models like Gemini 2.5 Pro at any time.
